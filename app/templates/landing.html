Why We Built It

The system wasn't built for patients — so we built one that is.

Drug manufacturers already run assistance programs that give away free medication — but they hide behind fax machines, confusing 400% Federal Poverty Guideline math, and forms written for hospital staff, not patients.

As we looked closer at prescription pricing, it became clear the savings were rarely the hard part. FDA "AB"-rated bioequivalent medicines are medically identical to their brand-name counterparts, yet patients are rarely told they exist at the pharmacy counter.

Remedi exists to close that gap. It puts the same lookups and paperwork a clinical advocate would do directly in the hands of everyday patients and families — no insurance broker or case manager required.
